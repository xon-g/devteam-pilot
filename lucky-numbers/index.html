Totoo ba ang Lucky Numbers?

Diretsahan na tayo: hindi. Walang numerong mas suwerte kaysa sa iba pagdating sa lotto. Gusto pa rin namin ang lucky numbers, kaya nga ginawa namin ang app na ito, pero mahalagang malinaw sa'yo kung bakit laro lang talaga ito.

Bawat draw ay bagong simula

Isipin mo ang Swertres. Bawat draw, may 1,000 posibleng resulta mula 000 hanggang 999, at pare-pareho ang tsansa ng bawat isa. Ang makina o draw system ay walang alaala. Hindi nito alam kung ano ang lumabas kahapon, noong isang linggo, o noong isang taon. Kaya kahit lumabas ang 7-7-7 kagabi, ang tsansa nitong lumabas ulit ngayong gabi ay 1 sa 1,000 pa rin. Ganoon din para sa kahit anong ibang combination.

Walang numerong "mainit" o "malamig"

Marami tayong naririnig sa kanto: "Mainit ang 3 ngayon," o "Matagal nang hindi lumalabas ang 18, lalabas na 'yan." Pero sa random na draw, walang numerong "hot" at walang numerong "due" o "malapit nang lumabas." Kung matagal nang hindi lumalabas ang isang numero, hindi ibig sabihin na may utang ang draw sa'yo. Ito ang tinatawag na gambler's fallacy, ang paniniwalang nagbabalanse ang suwerte sa paglipas ng panahon. Sa maikling panahon, natural lang na may mga numerong mas madalas lumabas at may mga numerong matagal hindi lumalabas. Iyan ang itsura ng tunay na random.

Eh, paano 'yung mga birthday at panaginip?

Okay lang gamitin ang birthday ng nanay mo, plate number, o numerong napanaginipan mo. Masaya 'yan at may personal na kahulugan. Pero tandaan: pareho lang ang tsansa nila sa kahit anong ibang numero. Ang tanging epekto ng birthday numbers ay mas maraming tao ang pumipili ng 1 hanggang 31, kaya kung manalo ka man, baka mas marami kang kahati sa jackpot. Hindi nito pinapataas ang tsansa mong manalo.

Kaya ano ang ginagawa ng app namin?

Simple lang: random na numero. Walang secret formula, walang pag-aaral ng past results, walang "sistema." Ang numerong ibibigay namin ay kasing-suwerte lang ng numerong pipiliin mo mismo, hindi mas mataas, hindi mas mababa. Ang silbi nito ay gawing mas masaya at mas mabilis ang pagpili, lalo na kung wala kang maisip na numero.

Kung may website o tao na nagsasabing kaya nilang sabihin kung ano ang lalabas, mag-ingat ka. Walang makakagawa niyan nang tapat.

Are Lucky Numbers Real?

Let's be straight with you: no. No number is luckier than any other when it comes to lotto. We still like lucky numbers, which is why we made this app, but it's important that you're clear on why it's only a game.

Every draw is a fresh start

Think about Swertres. Every draw has 1,000 possible results from 000 to 999, and each one has the same chance. The machine or draw system has no memory. It doesn't know what came out yesterday, last week, or last year. So even if 7-7-7 came out last night, its chance of coming out again tonight is still 1 in 1,000. The same goes for any other combination.

No number is "hot" or "cold"

We hear a lot on the street: "3 is hot right now," or "18 hasn't come out in a long time, it's about to." But in a random draw, no number is "hot" and no number is "due" or "about to come out." If a number hasn't come out in a long time, it doesn't mean the draw owes you. This is called the gambler's fallacy, the belief that luck balances out over time. Over a short period, it's natural for some numbers to come out more often and others to stay away for a long time. That's what real randomness looks like.

So what about birthdays and dreams?

It's okay to use your mom's birthday, a plate number, or a number you dreamed of. That's fun and personally meaningful. But remember: they have the same chance as any other number. The only effect of birthday numbers is that more people pick 1 to 31, so if you do win, you may have more people to share the jackpot with. It doesn't raise your chance of winning.

So what does our app do?

It's simple: random numbers. No secret formula, no study of past results, no "system." The number we give you is exactly as lucky as a number you'd pick yourself, no higher, no lower. Its use is to make picking more fun and quicker, especially when you can't think of a number.

If a website or a person claims they can tell you what will come out, be careful. No one can honestly do that.
