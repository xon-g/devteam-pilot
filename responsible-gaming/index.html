Responsible Gaming

To us, lotto should stay entertainment, like watching a movie or playing a mobile game. It's money spent for fun, not a way to earn or to pay off debts. So here are a few reminders we hope you take seriously.

18 and over only

You must be 18 years old or older to bet on PCSO games. The same goes for this app: it's for adults only. If you're younger, thanks for visiting, but this isn't for you right now.

Set a limit before you bet

  • Budget first. Before you go to the outlet, decide how much you can afford to lose this week, and don't go over it.
  • Don't use money meant for bills. Money for food, rent, electricity, tuition or medicine is not for betting.
  • Don't borrow to bet. If you have to borrow just to place a bet, that's a sign you need to stop.
  • Don't chase losses. Betting more to "win it back" usually ends in bigger losses. Every draw is random; there's no draw that will "make it up."
  • Set a time limit. You don't have to bet on every draw. Take a break too.

Signs it may be a problem

Think about whether you notice these: you're always thinking about betting, you hide from your family how much you spend, you feel stressed or irritated when you can't bet, or your bets keep getting bigger to feel the same excitement. You're not alone, and there's no shame in asking for help.

Where to get help

  • PAGCOR — PAGCOR has a Responsible Gaming program, including self-exclusion, which lets you ban yourself from gaming venues and platforms. Visit the responsible gaming page on PAGCOR's official website [CHECK: exact URL and hotline number].
  • PCSO — PCSO has information and contact channels for questions about their games. Check their official website or help page [CHECK: exact URL and hotline number].
  • Mental health support — if you feel intense stress, sadness or anxiety because of betting or debt, you can call the National Center for Mental Health crisis hotline [CHECK: current number, e.g. 1553].
  • Family and friends — sometimes the best first step is to talk to someone you trust.

Our role

This app only gives random numbers, for fun. We don't accept bets, we don't sell tickets, and we don't improve your chances of winning. If you notice that betting is becoming a burden in your life, it's better to close the app and take a rest.
