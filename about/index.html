About Us

Hi! Lotto Lucky Numbers PH is a free, simple web app that generates random lucky numbers for the popular lotto games in the Philippines: Swertres, EZ2, STL, 4D, 6D, and Lotto 6/42, 6/45, 6/49, 6/55 and 6/58. It used to be known as the Swertres lucky number generator, and we expanded it to cover more games.

Who made this?

It was made by one independent creator, just one person who loves building small, fun web tools. It's part of xonicbox, a small collection of for-fun websites. There's no big company behind it, no office, and no call center. It's just a side project made with love.

What we are NOT

This is important, so we'll make it clear:

  • We are not PCSO. We are not connected to, partnered with, or endorsed by the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office or any government agency. We only use the names of the games so you know what each number is for.
  • We are not a lotto outlet. We don't accept bets, we don't sell tickets, and we don't pay out prizes.
  • We don't forecast results. Every number the app shows is random. It does not improve your chances of winning.

Why did we make it?

Because it's fun! Many Filipinos have a ritual for picking numbers: birthdays, dreams, plate numbers, numbers on receipts. This app is one more fun way to pick, especially when your mind goes blank at the outlet. We also want to be honest: it's only random, and lotto should stay entertainment.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no fee and no account needed. In the future, we may add ads to help cover hosting costs. If that happens, we'll explain it in our Privacy Policy.

This is for ages 18 and up only. Have a question or suggestion? Visit our Contact page.
