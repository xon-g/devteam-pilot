Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information Lotto Lucky Numbers PH at https://lotto.xonicbox.com collects (and does not collect), and how we use it. We follow the spirit of the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we do NOT collect

The app may ask for a name, age, or other details to make the game more personal. We never send or store on a server the name, age, or anything you type in the app. It is used only on your device to generate a random number. If you pick a language, your browser remembers it on this device only (local storage) and it is never sent anywhere. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for an email, phone number, or address to use the app.

Anonymous usage stats (GoatCounter)

To find out how many people use the site and which pages are visited most, we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It only collects anonymous, aggregate counts, such as:

  • How many times a page was visited
  • What type of browser or device (e.g. mobile or desktop)
  • Which website you came from (referrer), if any
  • Approximate country

GoatCounter uses no cookies, and it doesn't build a profile about you or follow you to other websites. We can't tell who you are from this data.

Ads and third-party cookies (in the future)

Right now, the site may not have ads yet. But in the future, we may add ads to help cover hosting costs. If that happens:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this site or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to show you ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • You can also opt out of other third-party vendors' personalized ads at https://www.aboutads.info

We will update this page before or at the same time ads begin.

Email

If you email us, we will only use your email address and the contents of your message to reply. We will not sell or share it.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal data we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. Since we collect almost no personal data, usually all we hold is your email if you emailed us. To exercise these rights, email hello@xonicbox.com. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Children

This site is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy: hello@xonicbox.com
