How to Play

Before you generate lucky numbers, it helps to know how each game works. This is our quick guide to the PCSO games included in the app. One reminder: we are not PCSO, so always double-check the latest draw schedule, prices and prizes on the official PCSO website or at the lotto outlet you go to. These sometimes change.

Swertres (3D Lotto)

You pick three digits, each from 0 to 9 (for example: 4-0-7). There are three draws every day, around 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM and 9:00 PM [CHECK].

  • Straight — all three digits must be right, in the exact order. Since there are 1,000 possible combinations (000 to 999), your chance is 1 in 1,000.
  • Rambolito — you win even if the order is different, as long as the digits are the same. With three different digits (e.g. 1-2-3), there are 6 possible orders, so 6 in 1,000. With a pair (e.g. 1-1-2), there are only 3 orders, so 3 in 1,000. The chance is higher, but the prize is smaller than Straight [CHECK: current prize amount].

EZ2 (2D Lotto)

Two numbers, each from 1 to 31. The draw times are the same as Swertres, three times a day [CHECK].

  • Straight — exact order. There are 31 × 31 = 961 combinations, so 1 in 961.
  • Rambolito — even if the order is reversed. If the two numbers are different, 2 in 961.

STL (Small Town Lottery)

STL is run by authorized operators in each province or area, so the games (such as STL Pares, Swer2, Swer3), draw times and prizes vary depending on where you are [CHECK: draw times and game variants in each area]. The odds depend on the format: if it's the same as Swertres, Straight is still 1 in 1,000; if it's a pair (two numbers from 1 to 40), 1 in 1,600 [CHECK: STL Pares range].

4D and 6D

  • 4D — four digits, 0 to 9 each. 10,000 combinations, so 1 in 10,000 for the exact order. Drawn every Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 9:00 PM [CHECK].
  • 6D — six digits. 1,000,000 combinations, so 1 in 1,000,000 for the jackpot. There are small prizes for the last few digits [CHECK: prize tiers]. Drawn every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, 9:00 PM [CHECK].

Lotto 6/42, 6/45, 6/49, 6/55 and 6/58

You pick six numbers. Order doesn't matter here; all you need is for all six to be right. The draw is around 9:00 PM [CHECK].
